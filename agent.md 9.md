## 5. Practice and revision system

**Why this exists:** in your last attempt you did very little practice in Maths, English and GK/GS, and you forgot what you had learned. This plan fixes both: practice comes first, and everything you learn comes back on a schedule.

### 5.1 Principles
1. **Practice first.** Learn a concept, then practise it the same day.
2. **Revise by testing, not re-reading.** Every revision is a short quiz or a recall from memory.
3. **Every topic returns five times**, at 1, 3, 7, 21 and 45 days after you learn it.
4. **Revision is protected.** Due revision ranks above new concepts when time is short (section 9.3).

### 5.2 Time share (of subject hours, excluding Saturday mock and analysis)

| Phase | Learn | Practice | Revise | Hours/week (Learn / Practice / Revise) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (W1–8) | 35% | 40% | 25% | 6.8 / 7.8 / 4.9 |
| 2 (W9–16) | 15% | 55% | 30% | 2.9 / 10.7 / 5.9 |
| 3 (W17–24) | 5% | 50% | 45% | 0.7 / 7.3 / 6.5 (mocks take about 8 hrs) |

Practice includes quick quizzes (GA, vocab, English rules). Weekday quizzes happen at night; weekends carry about 65% of the weekly practice volume.

### 5.3 Revision layers

| Layer | When | Length | Tag | What you do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daily micro-revision | Every day | 10–15 min | [Commute] audio, [Break] quiz, then [Home] | Listen to the recap of topics due today and answer aloud; a phone quiz at a break if you have one; at night, 5 questions on yesterday's topic |
| Spaced queue | Every day | up to 30 min | [Commute] audio / [Home] | Topics due at D+1, D+3, D+7, D+21, D+45 (5.4). Question steps are done at night or on the weekend |
| Weekly revision block | Sunday afternoon | 90 min | Fixed time | Active recall of the whole week (5.5) |
| Monthly consolidation | 4th (light) week | 2 half-days | Fixed time | 100-question cumulative test and parking-lot catch-up (5.6) |
| Retention check | Every 14 days | 20 min | [Home] / weekend | 20 random questions from old topics (5.7) |

### 5.4 Spaced queue
| Step | After learning | Task |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 1 | 10 min: flashcards and 5 questions |
| 2 | Day 3 | 10 min: 5 questions |
| 3 | Day 7 | 15 min: 10 questions |
| 4 | Day 21 | 20 min: 15 questions |
| 5 | Day 45 | 20 min: 15 timed questions |

- Score below 70% on any step: the topic goes back to step 1 and is marked weak.
- The queue has a daily cap of 30 min. Extra items spread across the rest of the week, and due revision is never pushed more than 2 days.

### 5.5 Weekly revision block (Sunday, 90 min)
- **Quant:** rewrite the week's formulas from memory, then 15 mixed questions.
- **English:** recall the week's grammar rules and 20 error-spotting or fill-in questions.
- **GA:** 50-question quiz on the week's topics and current affairs.
- **Reasoning:** 10 mixed questions.
- Mark every miss in the mistake log (section 12).

### 5.6 Monthly consolidation (light week)
- Day 1: 100-question cumulative test (Quant 30, English 30, GA 30, Reasoning 10) covering the whole 4-week block.
- Day 2: analysis, repair, and the parking lot (section 9.4).

### 5.7 Retention check
Every 14 days, 20 random questions from topics learned more than 14 days ago. Below 70% on a topic puts it back in the queue as weak.

### 5.8 Notes you make as you learn
After each topic (5 min): a 5-line recap and one formula or rule card. These feed the daily micro-revision and the Sunday block, so you never have to re-watch a lecture.

### 5.9 Forgetting guard
A topic untouched for 21 days is marked red on the topic list and added to the next Sunday block.

### 5.10 GA and English specifics
- **GA:** 10 current-affairs points a day, 10 static-GK flashcards a day, a quiz on each SSC Parmar topic the same day, and a Saturday evening recap. Monthly 100-question GA quiz.
- **English:** one spelling or vocab set a day, one error-spotting set three times a week, one cloze passage and one reading-comprehension passage a week from week 2 on.
- **Maths:** every concept ends with at least 20 questions, then returns at the spaced steps.

### 5.11 Practice ladder
Untimed basics, then timed sets (about 36 seconds a question), then PYQs, then sectional tests, then full mocks. A topic moves up a step when accuracy is 80% or higher on 20 questions. Wrong answers are retried after 3 days.

### 5.12 Complete syllabus: learn, practise and revise everything
Your CGL 2026 paper is 100 questions. The syllabus is much bigger, and 46 of those questions you got right. So **the plan covers the whole syllabus, not only your mistakes.**

1. **Every syllabus topic gets all three tracks:** learn (or skim, if you are already strong), practise, and revise on the spaced schedule. No topic is skipped because you answered it correctly last time.
2. **Your paper sets the order and the extra time, never the limit.** Mistakes move a topic earlier and give it more practice; they never decide what is left out.
3. **Four topic groups, tagged from your full-paper log (section 12):**

| Group | Meaning | Rule | Share of practice time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Repair** | Wrong or skipped in your paper | Re-learn, then 20+ questions, then the spaced steps | 40% |
| **Speed** | Right in your paper | Timed drills to cut the time per question while keeping accuracy | 25% |
| **Maintain** | Strong topics (about 85%+ in practice) | Short timed sets and the spaced revision steps | 15% |
| **New to you** | Not in your paper at all (for example time and work, boats and streams, mixture, tenses, active and passive, coding–decoding, geometry circles) | Full learn, practise and revise | 20% |

4. **Speed track (for the 46 you answered correctly):**
   - Drill the same topics with timed sets. Target: at least 85% accuracy at an average of about 36 seconds a question, which is what 15 minutes for 25 questions allows.
   - Quick targets by section: Reasoning about 30 s, English about 36 s, GA about 20 s, Quant about 45 s with 3–4 skipped.
   - Practise method as well as the answer: for each topic, write the shortest way to solve it in one line.
5. **Time per question:** your response sheets do not show time per question, so the **baseline test on Sat 5 Dec** sets your first timing. From then on every test and timed set records the time per question or per set.
6. **Paper replay (your own questions again):**

| Replay | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Weeks 1–4, one section a week (on Sunday) | Redo the section's 25 questions at 15 min. First the 46 you got right (speed), then the 54 you missed, after they have been repaired |
| 2 | Weeks 9–12 | The whole paper again at 15 min per section |
| 3 | Weeks 17–20 | The whole paper once more, with explanations written from memory |

You have already seen these questions, so a replay score is **not a fair measure of ability**. Judge a replay by speed and by whether you can explain each solution in one line. The questions stay in your own response sheets; the planner only schedules the replay and records time and result per question.

7. **Coverage tracker:** for every syllabus topic the planner shows four ticks: Learned, Practised, Revised, Timed. The weekly review lists topics with fewer than four ticks.

### 7.4 Remove a task: exactly two choices
When you tap **Remove** on a task (or on several selected tasks, or on a whole day), the agent shows two options and nothing else:

**Option 1: Move it within this week**
The agent lists where it can go, and you pick one:
- **Next day** (if there is one left in this week)
- **An easier day** this week: the Easy day or the day with the most spare minutes, shown as "Wed, Easy, 15 min free"
- **Pick a day yourself**

Rules for Option 1:
1. The task stays in the **same Monday–Sunday week**. It can only go to today or a later day, never a past day or the next week.
2. If a day has no room, the agent shows how many minutes over it would be and lets you confirm.
3. If no day left this week has room (for example, it is Sunday), Option 1 is greyed out with the message "No room left this week", and you can only remove it completely.
4. A moved task is pinned to its new day.

**Option 2: Remove completely**
- The task is deleted. It does **not** go to the parking lot and does **not** appear in the Sunday review.
- A short undo option is shown, and the change log keeps a record.
- If it was the only task for a syllabus topic, the topic is marked "not covered yet" as information only. You can ignore it.
- If it was a due revision step (section 5.4), that step is marked skipped and the topic's later steps stay as they are.
- If it was a Floor task, the agent warns that the day now has fewer than 3 must-do tasks, and lets you continue.

- **Default start: Tue 1 Dec 2026.** You can change it before the plan begins, and again at any time after.
- Any start weekday works; a Monday start is not required. Weeks always run **Monday to Sunday**, so the Saturday mock and Sunday repair day stay in place.
- If the start date is not a Monday, the days up to the first Sunday form a **kickoff week (Week 0)**. The 24 full weeks start on the next Monday.

| Start date | Week 0 | Week 1 starts | 24 weeks end (about) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tue 1 Dec 2026 (default) | Tue 1 Dec – Sun 6 Dec | Mon 7 Dec | Sun 23 May 2027 |
| Mon 30 Nov 2026 | none | Mon 30 Nov | Sun 16 May 2027 |
| Mon 14 Dec 2026 | none | Mon 14 Dec | Sun 30 May 2027 |

Starting earlier than 1 Dec (for example 23 Nov) stretches Week 0 back to that date; starting later shrinks it.

**Kickoff week (default content):** set up the planner, tag the 54 mistakes (about 90 min total, split into chunks), build the formula card, and take a full Tier 1 baseline test on Saturday 5 Dec with 15 minutes per section. Day types follow the real weekday of each date.

### 8.2 Changing the date later

| Action | What it does | What stays the same |
|---|---|---|
| **Move start date** (before starting) | Re-draws every date, mock Saturday, light week and phase boundary | Topic order, tasks, mistake log |
| **Shift plan** (after starting) | Moves all future weeks earlier or later by whole weeks, from a chosen Monday | Completed days, scores, mistake log |
| **Insert gap** | Pauses from a date for N days or weeks and moves everything after it | Past data and topic order |
| **Set exam date** (optional) | Counts back from the exam, then compresses or stretches the plan (minimum 12 weeks) | Phase order; Phase 3 mocks stay last |
| **Change plan length** (20–32 weeks) | Re-plans every week from Week 1 (section 8.3) | Ticks, scores, mistake log, your own tasks |
| **Undo last change** | Restores the previous plan version | |

Rules:
1. Shifts happen in **whole weeks** where possible, so a week is never split across two plan weeks.
2. A shift never removes completed work or a logged mistake.
3. Phase lengths are one third each (8/8/8 for 24 weeks) and change with the plan length (section 8.3), or when an exam date forces a change; then the agent shortens Phase 2 first, never the Phase 3 mocks.
4. Every change goes to a **change log** (date, what changed, why).
5. Before applying a change, show a preview: new Week 1 date, new end date, and weeks gained or lost.

### 8.3 Plan length (about 5 to 7 months) and full re-plan
The plan is **24 weeks by default, about 5.5 months**. You can change the length at any time to anything from **20 to 32 weeks** (about 4.6 to 7.4 months). A 7-month plan is about 30 weeks.

| 24 weeks (default) | 5.5 months | 8 / 8 / 8 | Sun 23 May 2027 | about 540 |
| 26 weeks | 6.0 months | 9 / 9 / 8 | Sun 6 Jun 2027 | about 585 |
| 28 weeks | 6.4 months | 9 / 10 / 9 | Sun 20 Jun 2027 | about 630 |
| 30 weeks | 6.9 months (about 7) | 10 / 10 / 10 | Sun 4 Jul 2027 | about 675 |
| 32 weeks | 7.4 months | 11 / 11 / 10 | Sun 18 Jul 2027 | about 720 |

(Start on Tue 1 Dec adds the kickoff week before Week 1.)

**When you change the length, the agent re-plans every week from Week 1.** It re-derives:
1. **Phases:** about one third each (table above).
2. **Topic pace:** topics are spread over all the weeks, so a longer plan means fewer new topics a week and more practice and revision per topic.
3. **Light weeks and monthly blocks:** re-spaced (a light week about every 4th week; 6 monthly blocks for 24 weeks, 7 for 28–30, 8 for 32).
4. **Weekly and monthly targets:** re-set for each week.
5. **Mock milestones:** kept at the same fractions of the plan: first full mock and 100/200 at one third, 115 at one half, 130 at two thirds, 145 at five sixths, and 155+ at the end.
6. **Tier 2 Paper I track:** starts after the first third of the plan (week 9 in a 24-week plan, week 11 in a 30-week plan).
7. **Sunday revision, spaced revision and the weekly hours:** unchanged. Only the total number of weeks changes.

**Re-plan scope (you choose in the preview):**
- **A. Full re-plan from Week 1 (default):** every week is re-planned for the whole timeline. Ticks, minutes, mock scores, mistakes and revision history you already have stay attached; a done task is matched to its topic in the new plan.
- **B. From this week onward:** weeks already finished stay exactly as they were.

Rules:
1. Before applying, show a **preview**: new end date, phase lengths, topics per week, total hours, and what moves.
2. The agent saves a **snapshot first**, so you can undo the re-plan (section 20).
3. Your own tasks (added or pinned, section 7) are kept on their dates when possible; if a date no longer exists, the agent asks where to put them.
4. Shortening to fewer weeks warns that the pace goes up and lists the topics that get compressed.
5. If an exam date is set, the agent warns when the new length runs past it.
6. Extending is easiest before week 20, because there is more room to re-spread topics.

## 9. Weekly buckets and same-week carry-over

Some days you will get very little study time. This section controls what happens to tasks you don't finish.

### 9.1 Core rules
1. **A week is a closed bucket (Mon–Sun).** Every task belongs to one week and must finish within it.
2. **Tasks move within the week, never past it.** A task missed on Tuesday can move to Wednesday, Thursday and so on up to Sunday. Nothing from Week N is added to Week N+1.
3. **Tasks are stored as small chunks** (10–25 min). A 60-min concept task is 3–4 chunks, so leftovers can be spread thinly instead of dumped on one day.
4. **Spread, don't stack.** Leftover chunks are shared across the remaining days of that week in proportion to each day's spare capacity.
5. **You are in control.** Anything the engine moves can be moved back, and tasks you add or move yourself are pinned (section 7.1).

### 9.2 How leftover time is spread

| Day | Extra it can absorb |
|---|---|
| Easy | up to +15 min |
| Moderate | up to +20 min |
| Hard | up to +15 min (hard days are protected) |
| Saturday (mock day) | up to +45 min, after the mock block |
| Sunday (repair day) | up to +60 min |

- Floor tasks are never pushed more than 2 days.
- Audio [Commute] chunks are placed first, so a busy day doesn't block catch-up; chunks that need a screen go to a night or the weekend.
- Moves happen automatically after the nightly check-in; the day shows "+2 chunks moved from Tue".

### 9.3 Priority when time is short
Highest first: Saturday mock and analysis, Floor tasks, due revision (section 5), logging mistakes, timed practice, new concepts, Stretch practice. Lower items are dropped first. Due revision is never pushed more than 2 days.

### 9.4 End of week (Sunday 23:59, the week locks)
- Unfinished chunks are **not carried to the next week**. They are marked **Dropped**.
- The agent shows a weekly summary: planned vs done, dropped chunks by subject, and the 3 topics affected.
- Dropped chunks go to a **parking lot**. By default it is shown only in the **catch-up slot of the light week** (4th week of each block) and in Phase 3 revision, so no regular week is ever overloaded.
- **Sunday review question:** each Sunday the agent lists the dropped chunks and asks, per subject: **Park** (default) or **Drop**. If you don't answer by 23:59 Sunday, they are parked.
- Dropped (not parked) chunks are only flagged "revisit" on the topic.

### 9.5 When you have very little time today
1. Choose **Floor**, or enter the minutes you really have.
2. The agent keeps the highest-priority chunks that fit.
3. The rest are spread over the remaining days of the week (9.2).
4. If the week has no room left, the agent shows the minutes over and offers: **drop** low-priority chunks, **compress** (shorter sets), or **park** them for the light-week catch-up.

### 9.6 Example
Tuesday (Hard, Target 2.5 hr) = 150 min planned. You only get 75 min.
- 75 min of chunks are left.
- Spare capacity this week: Wed +15, Thu +20, Fri +15, Sat +45, Sun +60 = 155 min.
- Spread: Wed 15, Thu 20, Fri 15, Sat 15, Sun 10 = 75 min, all inside the week.
- If Wed to Fri also go badly, Sunday absorbs what it can; the rest is dropped to the parking lot at 23:59 Sunday.

## 10. Breaks, festivals, leave and long study breaks

### 10.1 Day modes

| Mode | Study time | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Off** | 0 | Festival, family event, travel, illness, full rest day |
| **Floor only** | Floor tasks | Half-festival days, very tired, work crunch |
| **Light** | Easy-day level | Return after a break, low energy |
| **Bonus** | Stretch or weekend-style | A leave day or holiday you choose to use |

Mark a day in advance (calendar view) or on the fly. An Off day is not "missed", doesn't break any streak, and never triggers a guilt message.

### 10.2 What each break does

| Break | Effect on the week | Effect on the plan |
|---|---|---|
| 1 day Off | That day's chunks spread inside the same week (section 9); week capacity drops | None |
| 2–4 days (festival, travel) | Week is rescaled to the days left; low-priority chunks (extra practice, revision) drop first; Saturday mock stays unless also off | None, or a note if a topic was cut |
| Whole week Off | Marked **Break week** | End date moves by 1 week; everything after shifts forward |
| Long study break (2+ weeks) | Weeks marked Break | End date moves by the break length; a **re-entry week** follows (10.3) |
| Work crunch (3+ Floor days) | Agent switches the week to Floor and replans on Saturday | None |

### 10.3 Re-entry after a break
- **1–6 days:** the first day back is Light, then the normal plan resumes.
- **7+ days:** the first week back is a **Re-entry week**: Floor and Easy-level tasks, revision of the last 2 weeks' topics, and a short Saturday sectional.
- **3+ weeks:** add a baseline sectional before restarting; the agent re-ranks weak topics.
- Topics are never skipped silently. A topic cut from a week moves with the shifted schedule.

### 10.4 Festival and leave planning
- The agent does **not** hard-code festival dates, and calendar import is off. You enter festivals, leave and breaks yourself in the calendar view.
- For each festival day you choose Off, Floor only or Light.
- Five days before a planned break, the agent suggests a **pre-break week**: finish the Hard tasks early, then relax.
- A leave day set to **Bonus** adds one extra Hard task from that week.

### 10.5 Never-guilt rules
- Off days never lower your weekly score.
- Weekly target = Target hours × (available days ÷ 7).
- A festival week where you meet the scaled target counts as a successful week.

## 17. Agent rules

1. Office work comes first. Never plan study that needs you to skip work or break workplace rules.
2. Weekdays have no fixed clock times; every task has a tag and a tier.
3. Commute tasks are audio only, because you walk. Floor tasks must be doable by audio or on a phone at home. Break quizzes are phone-only, optional, and never required for Floor; plan no laptop or desk study at the office.
4. Task minutes must equal the chosen tier's total.
5. Every task has a syllabus tag and fits its day type.
6. No two Hard days in a row; every 4th week is light.
7. Quant gets the largest block, but the agent rotates which subject leads each day.
8. GA and vocab live mostly in commute time.
9. Reasoning stays at about 1.5 hrs/week unless its accuracy drops below 85%.
10. After every Saturday mock, re-rank weak topics and rewrite next week's plan.
11. If two consecutive weeks fall below Floor, shrink the plan; never pile up a backlog.
12. Do not invent exam dates, cutoffs, links, or video numbers.
13. Keep data on the user's device; share nothing unless the user chooses.
14. Be direct, not harsh: show progress and gaps with numbers, and no guilt messages for missed days.
15. A week is a closed Monday–Sunday bucket: chunks may move to later days in the same week, never to the next week.
16. Dropped chunks go to the parking lot and appear only in a light-week catch-up or Phase 3 revision, unless the user turns that off.
17. Shifts and breaks move the plan in whole weeks where possible, keep topic order, and never delete completed work or logged mistakes.
18. Show a preview and write to the change log before applying any date change.
19. Weekly targets scale with available days; Off days never count as missed.
20. Do not use connectors; festivals, leave and breaks are entered by the user.
21. Practice and revision must never fall below the shares in section 5.2; if time is short, cut new concepts first.
22. Every topic gets the five spaced revision steps; a score below 70% resets the topic to step 1.
23. At the end of each week, ask the user to park or drop unfinished chunks; default is park.
24. The task list is never locked: the user can add, edit, move or remove any task at any time.
25. Removing a task offers exactly two choices: move it within the same week (next day, an easier day, or a day the user picks) or remove it completely. No other automatic outcome.
26. Manual moves are pinned; the agent never overrides a user edit.
27. Removing a task never sends it to the parking lot; only the Sunday review does that.
28. Changing the plan length re-plans every week from Week 1, shows a preview, and saves a snapshot first; completed work is never lost.
29. The page must work on phone and laptop, save progress after every change, and never fail to save silently.
30. The agent tells the user at build time whether sync is available, and whether the build must happen outside an Incognito chat.
31. Cover the whole syllabus. Mistakes set priority and extra time; they never remove a topic from the plan.
32. Questions the user got right are used for speed drills and paper replays, not skipped.
33. A replay score is not a fair ability measure; judge replays by speed and method.

## 20. Saving your progress (storage and backup)

Progress means: ticked tasks and minutes, tasks you added or moved, mock scores, the mistake log, the revision history, plan settings (start date, length, breaks), notes and the change log.

| Layer | What it does | Protects against |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Autosave** | Saves to the browser after every change (checking a box, adding a task, logging a mistake) | Closing the tab, a dropped connection |
| **2. Snapshots** | Keeps the last 10 versions, and always saves one before a re-plan, shift, bulk edit or import | A change you regret |
| **3. Export and import** | One button saves everything to a file (JSON); another restores from it | Clearing browser data, a new phone or laptop, switching browsers |
| **4. Backup reminder** | Shows "last backed up N days ago" and nudges you every Sunday during the weekly review | Forgetting to back up |
| **5. Sync across devices** | Keeps the phone and laptop on the same data, if the hosting platform provides shared storage for your account | Using two devices |

Rules:
1. **Autosave is instant and silent**; a small "Saved" marker confirms it. If saving fails, a clear banner appears at once. The page never fails silently.
2. If the browser opens with no data but a snapshot or backup exists, the page offers to restore it.
3. **Merging two devices (when sync is on):** per task, the most recent change wins, but a task marked done is never reverted to not done by an older change.
4. Browser storage belongs to one browser on one device. Clearing site data or using a private window can erase it, so **export weekly** if sync is not available.
5. Sync is **not guaranteed yet**. At build time the agent checks whether shared storage is available on your account and tells you, so you know whether phone and laptop will stay in sync or you must use Export and Import.
6. If sync uses shared storage, keep the page link private. The agent confirms at build time who can see the data.
7. Data size is small (a few hundred KB), so storage limits are not a concern.
8. Offline use is a goal. The agent checks at build time what the hosting allows and says so.
